# The 1807 brand asset map

Only approved owner-supplied marks may represent The 1807 or its products.

| Asset | Approved use | Do not use for | Background and size |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/assets/images/img/logo/logo.png` | Primary company logo in headers, footers, brand introductions, and social metadata | Generic interface controls or product logos | Use on dark or neutral backgrounds; preserve legibility and do not render below 96 CSS pixels wide |
| `/assets/images/img/logo/logooss.png` | Minimal shark for the loader, compact brand moments, and the puzzle image | Repeated decoration or a replacement for the full header logo | Prefer dark backgrounds; render at least 32 CSS pixels |
| `/assets/images/img/favicons/favicon.ico` | Default browser favicon | In-page decoration | Browser-managed favicon sizes |
| `/assets/images/img/logo/logoos.png` | Expressive shark for puzzle completion and rare high-impact transitions | Every section, navigation items, or generic buttons | Dark backgrounds; preserve the complete artwork |
| `/assets/images/img/favicons/favicon0.ico` | Expressive favicon variant for approved campaigns or transitions | Default favicon without an explicit design decision | Browser-managed favicon sizes |

## Product marks

Hydrion, Modoroco, and ClipSense use only their owner-supplied artwork and real interface imagery. Generic drops, clocks, cameras, emoji, icon-library symbols, and generated marks must not replace product branding.

Generic interface symbols are allowed only when text would be less clear, such as close, menu, download, or carousel controls.
